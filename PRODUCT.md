# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Homeowners, realtors, event hosts, farmers, contractors and small businesses in central and southwest Ohio (Mount Vernon and Oxford areas) who need aerial photos or video. They arrive comparing local drone operators and want to know quickly: is this person legit, what does it cost, and how do I book.

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for Altitude Images LLC, an FAA Part 107 certified, commercially insured drone photography and videography business. Success is a visitor picking a package and booking a session through the /book page.

## Brand Personality
Professional, rugged, trustworthy. Outdoorsy and dependable, anchored by the mountain logo mark. Confident without hype.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS landing pages, stock "drone company" templates, anything flashy or gimmicky that undercuts the sense of a dependable licensed operator.

## Design Principles
- Credibility first: credentials (Part 107, insurance) and clear pricing stay visible.
- Let the aerial photography carry the page; UI stays out of the way.
- Every path leads to booking.
- Keep the established navy and gold identity; new brand elements adapt to it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA contrast. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Must work well on phones, since many visitors come from social links.
