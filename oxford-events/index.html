FAQ

Questions From Org Leaders

Can you fly on campus?
I'm commercially insured and handle airspace and permissions for every location. I'll confirm what's possible for your venue before you book.
What if it rains or it's too windy?
Free reschedule, or a full refund if we can't find a new date.
How far ahead should we book?
On-campus events need at least two weeks' notice to get university flight approval. For off-campus events, two weeks is ideal, but message me even if your event is this weekend.
Do you fly over crowds?
I follow FAA rules on flying over people and plan shots to keep everyone safe. The footage still looks great.
How do we pay?
Venmo or check payable to Altitude Images LLC, due within 15 days of delivery.
